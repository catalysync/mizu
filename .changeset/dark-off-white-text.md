The dark sample theme's `--mizu-text-primary` is now off-white (slate-100, `#f1f5f9`) instead of pure white. Pure white on slate-950 (20:1) halates: thin text glows and smears for many readers. The new value keeps 18.4:1 on the dark surface (AAA) and a clear step above secondary text.
