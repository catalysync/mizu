## P01 Satisfiable peers

- Change: every internal `@mizunagara/*` peer range moved from `>=0.2.0` to `>=0.1.0`, which the
  current 0.1.0 versions satisfy and the 0.2.0 release also satisfies. Do not use `^0.1.0`: once css and tokens
  bump to 0.2.0, changesets treats the out-of-range peer as breaking and bumps every dependent to 1.0.0
  (seen in `changeset status --output`). `>=0.1.0` keeps the release at 0.2.0 with minor bumps.

  - After: exit 0.
- Check 2: `pnpm pack` in all nine publishable packages, then `npm i --prefer-offline` of the packed
  `tokens`, `css`, `tailwind-preset` and `react` tarballs in a clean temp dir.
  - Result: `added 74 packages`, no `npm warn` lines, `npm ls` shows no invalid or missing peers.
  - Note: the packs `charts`, `finance`, `commerce`, `query-builder` and `advanced-query-builder` have no
    `dist` yet, because they are not built. Their tarballs are metadata only. Build gating comes in P03 and P08.
